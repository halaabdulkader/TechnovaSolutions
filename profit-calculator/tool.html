VAT

If the price includes VAT: item price × VAT rate / (100 + VAT rate). If it does not: item price × VAT rate / 100. Shipping the customer pays is not taxed.

—

Revenue excluding VAT

Amount the customer pays − VAT

—

Card fee

Amount the customer pays × commission rate + fixed fee.

—

Profit before tax

Revenue excluding VAT − direct costs − card fee

—

Company tax

Profit before tax × tax rate, only when the profit is positive. A loss has zero tax.
