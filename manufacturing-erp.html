Typical Delivery Shape

Exact dates depend on discovery. This is the rhythm we use for the starter slice.

  1. 1

    Discovery (about 1 week)

    Map your real flow—orders, planning, stock, people. Lock the first slice.

  2. 2

    Blueprint & quote

    Data model, screens, permissions. Fixed delivery plan before build heats up.

  3. 3

    Build sprints (typically 6–10 weeks)

    Working software every sprint you can click—not a big-bang reveal.

  4. 4

    Launch & expand

    Go-live support, then optional modules: production, purchases, shipments, AI assist.
